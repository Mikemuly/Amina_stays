# Amina Stays — small-host (2–10 units) research brief (as of 25 Nov 2026)

## Summary
- Communication is a leading guest complaint but not #1. Property condition and cleanliness is #1. Hosts' own biggest time sinks are **cleaning/maintenance and admin**. Pair comms automation with turnover tasks and reporting.
- Most inbound questions are static facts. Wi-Fi, check-in, parking, directions and door code made up ≈75% of one host's pre-arrival messages. The winning pattern is to auto-answer facts, ask the host on permission requests, and keep problems human.
- Design traps: Airbnb off-platform rules, Kenya TRA licensing and monthly returns, and ODPC data-protection registration.
- Most figures are vendor-published. Weak or biased ones are marked ⚠.

## 1. Host pain points
**Priorities & time sinks**
- PriceLabs 2025 report (1,400+ hosts): the biggest time drains are cleaning/maintenance, admin/tax and platform-rule changes, not messaging. Hosts spend 8.3 h/week and only 10% plan turnover tech. 71% fear a bad review and 63% fear losing ranking. Only 14% say AI helps, and 43–47% find it overwhelming. "Cleaning" appeared 186 times in open answers. Hosts say reliable cleaning/maintenance, better guest vetting and clearer OTA rules would help most ([Host Report](https://www.thehostreport.com/news/survey-of-1-400-hosts-shows-ai-isnt-easing-the-load-admin-tasks-remain-the-biggest-burden), [summary](https://www.strassociationofsa.com/post/the-reality-of-hosting-in-2025-what-1-400-hosts-told-pricelabs)).
- Hostaway Summer 2025 (320 operators): maintenance is a top-3 challenge for 4 in 10. Property condition/cleanliness is the top guest complaint, ahead of communication, check-in access and amenities. Constant price adjustment and competition add pressure ([Hostaway](https://www.hostaway.com/blog/vacation-rental-trends-2025/)).
- Guesty-reported survey: priorities are occupancy/revenue 68% and maintenance 58%. The top occupancy drags are competition (66%), poor channel placement (21%), marketing spend (16%), reviews (16%) and too few channels (15%). 51% saw revenue rise and 26% saw it fall. 45% say one bad review outweighs dozens of good ones, and 39% struggle to get reviews ([PhocusWire](https://www.phocuswire.com/strs-occupancy-rates-revenue-priority-number-one)).
- Breezeway survey: >85% say guest expectations rose in two years, nearly half face last-minute issues daily, and comms are fragmented across 3–5 platforms ([Guesty](https://www.guesty.com/blog/breezeway-hospitality-operator-survey/)).
- Hospitable (n=554): costs hit 45.1% of respondents, and guest comms is the #1 AI use ([Hospitable](https://hospitable.com/reports/industry-report-2026)).

**Communication volume & FAQs**
- Touch Stay analysed 37,359 chatbot conversations across 11,293 properties (Nov 2025–May 2026). Wi-Fi was the #1 question. 15.2% of questions went unanswered because the info was missing (Wi-Fi, cancellation policy, hairdryer, iron, heating). 24.2% of conversations had negative sentiment (Wi-Fi down, no hot water, lockouts) ([Touch Stay](https://touchstay.com/blog/airbnb-wifi)).
- One host's audit of 500 messages: Wi-Fi 23%, early check-in 18%, parking 14%, directions 11%, door code 9%. 87% had static answers. Sending the guidebook link at confirmation cut pre-arrival messages ~80% (self-reported) ([Stay Pilot](https://www.stay-pilot.com/blog/analyzed-500-guest-messages-what-travelers-ask-before-checkin)).
- "70–80% are the same 20 questions" is unsourced vendor copy ⚠ ([Conduit](https://www.conduit.ai/blog/70-of-your-guest-messages-don-t-need-a-human-to-answer-them)).
- Touch Stay guest survey (n=2,024, 2024 study):
  - Two-thirds got ≤2 host messages, and >1/5 lacked needed info.
  - 69% say communication sways reviews.
  - 53% prefer email pre-stay and 44% prefer text mid-stay.
  - 67% want self check-in.
  - With 3–5 contacts, 66% felt "very well looked after".
  - Sources: [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/insights-innovations-from-guest-communication-study-touch-stay-0x9mc), [Proptonomy](https://proptonomy.ai/research/guest-communication-2026).
- Response time: Airbnb response rate is the % of new inquiries answered within 24 h over 30 days, and it affects search. Superhost needs 90% ([Airbnb 430](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/430), [829](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/829)). Vrbo reportedly wants a reply within 1 h on check-in day and in-stay. "Reply within 1 h ⇒ +25% conversion" and "9-min SMS average" are weakly sourced ⚠ ([Enso](https://ensoconnect.com/resources/response-times-in-hospitality-a-guide-for-vacation-rental-professionals)).

**Double bookings**
- iCal is polled, not pushed. Airbnb imports every 3 h and Refresh is rate-limited ([Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/99)). Booking.com imports every 2 h ([Booking.com](https://partner.booking.com/en-us/help/rates-availability/extranet-calendar/how-synchronize-your-calendars-across-channels)). Vrbo takes ~20–30 min ([Lodgix](https://www.lodgix.com/answers/ical-vs-api-calendar-sync/)).
- iCal carries only booked/blocked nights, with no rates or restrictions. API channel managers push near-real-time ([Wise Media](https://wisemedia.io/2026/10/17/channel-manager-direct-booking-website/)).
- Airbnb host-cancel fee: 10% (>30 d), 25% (48 h–30 d), 50% of unstayed nights (≤48 h), $50 minimum, plus a possible calendar block ([Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/990)).
- "≥25% of new Booking.com hosts double-book in year one" is secondary ⚠ ([Hostaway](https://www.hostaway.com/blog/airbnb-double-booking/)).

**Check-in, damage, parties, cancellations, payments**
- 74% of hosts offer self check-in and 68% use smart locks ([Lodgify](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/vacation-rental-hosts-betting-big-on-direct-bookings-to-power-2025-growth-new-lodgify-survey-reveals-302356257.html)).
- AirCover gives $3M damage cover, $1M liability and ID screening ([Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com.sg/resources/hosting-homes/a/how-aircover-for-hosts-works-469)). Most hosts may not take deposits or holds, and any deposit is due back within 7 days ([Airbnb policy](https://www.airbnb.com.mt/help/article/2799)).
- Party reports are down >50% since 2020, and <0.035% of 2024 stays had one ([Airbnb](https://news.airbnb.com/airbnb-rolls-out-anti-party-system-for-halloween)). No-show data: not found.
- Direct card bookings need 3-D Secure, authorise-then-capture, and a signed agreement, ID and check-in logs as dispute evidence ([MyVRP](https://www.myvrp.com/blog/protect-your-vacation-rental-from-fraud-and-chargebacks), [Zeevou](https://zeevou.com/blog/how-to-take-payments-for-direct-bookings/)).
- Kenya: Short Stays Kenya and Sikika sell M-Pesa (Daraja) direct booking, and Sikika auto-issues door codes ([Short Stays](https://www.shortstayskenya.com/mpesa-payments-for-airbnb), [Sikika](https://sikika-ke.co.ke/posts/airbnb-direct-booking-with-mpesa-and-door-codes-kenya)).
- Languages: 62.2% of EU platform nights were spent by international guests ([Proptonomy/Eurostat](https://proptonomy.ai/research/guest-communication-2026)). No Mombasa guest-mix data found.

**Kenya compliance**
- TRA Tourism Enterprises Regulations 2025 (L.N. 200) bar operating without a TRA licence, which expires 31 Dec. They also require **monthly returns** of bed occupancy, visitors by country of origin and revenue ([Kenya Law](https://kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2025/200)).
- Service-apartment fees: KSh 1,000 application plus 12,000/yr (1 unit), 20,000 (2), 29,000 (3–5), 38,000 (6–10). Late renewal costs 10%/month ([Business Radar](https://businessradar.co.ke/airbnb-tra-licence-kenya-2025-regulations/)).
- A 2% tourism levy and 16% VAT above KES 5M turnover come from a consultancy source, so verify ([Hisibati](https://hisibati-consulting.co.ke/airbnb-taxation-in-kenya-a-guide/)).
- Hospitality must register with the ODPC at any size ([PM Law](https://www.pmlaw.co.ke/insights/data-protection-registration-kenya-odpc)). Reported but unverified: a TRA guest register, plus a 15 Jan 2024 PSRA directive to record/temporarily hold guest ID and keep a vehicle log. Verify with counsel ([DPA guide](https://dataprivacyadvocates.co.ke/resources/registration-guides/hospitality/)).

**OTA fees & direct booking**
- Airbnb charges a single 15.5% host-only fee (PMS hosts since 27 Oct 2025) ([Hostfully](https://www.hostfully.com/blog/airbnb-simplified-pricing/)). Booking.com is ≈15% ([STR Intel](https://www.specrom.com/str-intel/insights/airbnb-15-5-percent-host-only-fee-rollout-timeline/)). Vrbo moves to a flat 12% from 29 Oct 2026, up from 5% (PMS) or 8% ([Expedia](https://www.expedia.com/newsroom/vrbo-compensation-model-changes/), [Skift](https://skift.com/2026/09/29/vrbo-to-double-commissions-as-it-plays-catch-up-to-airbnb/)).
- Direct was ≈34% of US bookings vs Airbnb 46% (2024). This is Lodgify customer data, so it is biased upward ⚠. 57% call driving direct bookings a top-3 challenge ([Lodgify](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/vacation-rental-hosts-betting-big-on-direct-bookings-to-power-2025-growth-new-lodgify-survey-reveals-302356257.html)).
- ⚠ Airbnb bans pre-booking off-platform contact, off-platform discounts and links, rebooking off-platform, and encouraging "repeat bookings" off-platform. Optional extras may be paid offline ([Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com.mt/help/article/2799), [guest-side](https://www.airbnb.com.sg/help/article/209)). One blog says repeat guests may be invited to book direct after a stay ([Rakidzich](https://www.rakidzich.com/articles/airbnb-off-platform-message-policy-what-you-can-say-2026)). The official text says otherwise, so treat this as prohibited for Airbnb-sourced guests.

## 2. Tooling ecosystem
**PMS (prices via third-party ProhostAI, 19 Nov 2026; verify)** ([ProhostAI](https://prohost.ai/blog/best-pms-for-small-short-term-rental-hosts))
- Hospitable: free Essentials plan (inbox, scheduled messages, tasks). Paid plans add automation, AI replies, pricing, a direct site and smart-lock automation. It also lists guest verification, rental agreements, security deposits and damage insurance ([Hospitable](https://hospitable.com/reports/industry-report-2026)).
- OwnerRez: $40 for the first property, then $15/$15/$10/$8. Owner statements, SMS and AI are add-ons.
- Lodgify: site, booking engine and 0% booking fees from Starter ($26). Automation is on Professional, and cleaning tasks and owner statements are on Ultimate.
- Guesty Lite (1–3 listings) starts at $20/listing. Hostaway has no public price and is not for single units. Hostfully is $15/property plus a fee. Uplisting has a ≤4-property plan. Smoobu is €29 plus a 0.9% booking fee.
- Guesty, Hospitable, OwnerRez, Lodgify and Hostfully are Airbnb "Preferred+" partners.
- AI: Guesty ReplyAI drafts replies (since 2024). Its Autopilot (22 Apr 2026) has a confidence threshold, per-property switch, active hours, category allow-list, sentiment escalation, knowledge upload and auto-translate. Hostaway has AI Replies plus opt-in Auto-Reply by topic ([EasilyData](https://www.easilydata.com/guides/guesty-replyai-vs-hostaway-ai-auto-reply/)). Hospitable's Inbox AI (Mogul tier) replies after hours only ([Trellis](https://www.trellistech.com/blog/best-unified-inbox-for-vacation-rentals-2026)).
- Core pattern: unified inbox → triggered messages with placeholders → AI draft → human escalation.
  - Airbnb's scheduled quick replies fire on new reservation, check-in or checkout. A toggle sends skipped messages immediately for last-minute bookings ([Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com.mt/help/article/2897)).
  - Hostaway upsells appear in the booking engine, guest portal and check-in form, with an auto-task on "Upsell added" ([Hostaway](https://support.hostaway.com/hc/en-us/articles/19918681033755-Listings-Manage-Custom-Fees-and-Upsells)).
- Adoption among Hospitable's audience: PMS essential 89.9%, dynamic pricing 71.3%, smart locks 48.9%. No USSD channel found in any source.

**Dynamic pricing rules**
- PriceLabs ([PriceLabs](https://hello.pricelabs.co/blog/pricelabs-dynamic-pricing-software-for-vacation-rentals/)):
  - A base price anchors everything, with min/max bounds and seasonal profiles.
  - Defaults are a gradual 30% last-minute discount over 15 days (configurable to 90) and a 20% orphan-day discount for 1–2-night gaps. The larger discount wins, so they don't stack.
  - Weekend days are redefinable (e.g. Thu/Fri in the Middle East). It also has minimum weekend/far-out prices, day-of-week adjustments and date overrides.
  - Min-stay precedence is date override > far-out > last-minute > default. The orphan rule applies only if it lowers min nights.
- Wheelhouse: gap rules (in-between/before/after, nights, weekdays, negative % = discount), with default discounts larger for shorter gaps and weekdays. It also has long-stay, last-minute, day-of-week, seasonality and Aggressive/Recommended/Conservative settings ([Wheelhouse](https://help.usewheelhouse.com/en/articles/6395562-how-do-i-adjust-pricing-for-gap-nights)).
- Fees are ≈1% of revenue, and Beyond is the most hands-off ⚠ ([Rakidzich](https://www.rakidzich.com/articles/pricelabs-vs-wheelhouse-vs-beyond-pricing-2026)).

**Smart locks**
- igloohome algoPIN: a cloud token plus algorithm yields a PIN with its validity built in, like a bank OTP. The lock verifies it offline, guests need no app, and the PIN can be shared by SMS/WhatsApp ([igloohome](https://www.igloohome.co/how-it-works)).
- igloohome API details ([API](https://docs.igloohome.co/home/api_access), [overview](https://docs.igloohome.co/home.md)):
  - Endpoint: POST `/home/devices/{id}/algopin/{hourly|daily|onetime|permanent}`.
  - Hourly covers 1–672 h and daily covers 29–367 days.
  - Times are hour-resolution ISO with offset. `variance` and `accessName` are required, and it returns `pin` + `pinId`.
  - There is a 30-day API trial, then a 402 error. Some device SKUs are unsupported (418). Bridge jobs are async with webhooks.
- A lock holds up to 199 active PINs ([igloohome](https://www.igloohome.co/blog/why-igloo-s-offline-first-access-tech-solves-the-dead-zone-problem)).
- TTLock: the developer app needs manual approval. It supports time-limited, single-use and custom passcodes, and custom ones need a gateway or Wi-Fi lock ([TTLock](https://cnopen.ttlock.com/document-pages/en/userGuide/getStarted)). Third-party notes say keypad passcodes work on Bluetooth-only locks and round to the hour. They must be used within 24 h of start and can't be revoked without a gateway ⚠ ([GitHub](https://github.com/LouisGolding/ttlock-guest-access/blob/main/README.md)).
- Nuki–Airbnb sends access info 72 h before by default ([Nuki](https://help.nuki.io/hc/en-001/articles/21283496989329-Connect-Nuki-and-Airbnb)). Schlage Encode Plus auto-codes can be adjusted for early/late ([Schlage](https://www.schlage.com/en/home/smart-locks/schlage-encode-plus-works-with.html)).
- Hospitable's lock pattern: a random or phone-based code is scheduled on the lock and active check-in→checkout. It uses a `%smartlock_code%` placeholder and reschedules when check-in changes ([Hospitable](https://help.hospitable.com/en/articles/8170325-smart-locks-access-codes-how-they-work)).

**Noise, screening, guidebooks**
- Minut: noise, device-count occupancy, motion, smoke and temp/humidity, with no audio or camera. It can auto-message guests via Airbnb. NoiseAware: decibels plus duration with an adaptive baseline ([STR Specialist](https://strspecialist.com/compare/minut-vs-noiseaware)). Typical alerts are 75–85 dB sustained 5+ min, and devices must be disclosed ⚠ ([TIOO](https://tioo.com/blog/noise-monitoring-short-term-rentals-noiseaware-minut-privacy/)).
- Autohost: ID forensics (2,500+ document types, 140+ countries), liveness, risk score, six tiers at $0.12–$7.25, and no damage cover. Truvi/Superhog: screening plus up to $5M damage protection. Prices conflict across sources ([STR Specialist](https://strspecialist.com/compare/autohost-vs-superhog), [StaySTRA](https://staystra.com/best-str-guest-screening-tools-2026-autohost-truvi-safely-chekin/)). "98% chargeback win rate" is vendor-reported ⚠.
- Guidebooks: a Hostfully case study across 400 listings reports calls −15% and revenue +6% ([Hostfully](https://www.hostfully.com/success-stories/improving-guest-experience/)). "Up to 61% fewer inquiries" is a weak claim ⚠ ([StaySTRA](https://staystra.com/best-digital-welcome-book-str-hosts-2026/)).

**Upsells**
- Enso audited 4,394 operators (2024) and found 82.4% offer none. But early check-in, late checkout and mid-stay clean were excluded, and the sample was 67% North America and 4% small hosts ([Enso](https://ensoconnect.com/resources/vacation-rental-upsells-data-and-trends-2025)). "8–30% revenue growth" is vendor-claimed ⚠.
- Vendor benchmarks:
  - Early check-in has 18–28% take-up at $35–75, and late checkout 14–22%. Offered at confirmation, conversion is 22–28% vs 8–12% the day before ([Jurny](https://blog.jurny.com/2026-str-upsell-revenue-benchmarks-what-are-you-actually-leaving-on-the-table)).
  - Conversion of 15–20% and ≈$120/booking. The store covers early/late, luggage, airport transfer, pet fee, parking, mid-stay clean, tours and gap nights ([ChargeAutomation](https://chargeautomation.com/hostaway-upsells-early-checkin/)).
  - Mid-stay clean suits stays >5–6 nights, and grocery stocking 1–2 days before arrival ([MagicBNB](https://magicbnb.io/blog/airbnb-upsells-revenue-per-stay)).
  - No breakfast-specific data found.

**Housekeeping**
- Turno auto-creates a clean from the calendar checkout. The cleaner accepts in-app, completes a photo-guided checklist, and is paid automatically. Its marketplace covers only the US, Canada, UK and Australia, so in Mombasa you'd bring your own cleaner.
- Breezeway has mandatory-photo inspections, scoring, issue escalation, work orders and owner reports.
- Pricing conflicts across sources: Turno free for ≤2 properties vs $10 for bring-your-own, and Breezeway $19.99/unit vs $125+/mo ([Trellis](https://www.trellistech.com/blog/breezeway-vs-turno-for-turnover-scheduling), [STRStack](https://strstack.io/comparisons/best-cleaning-software-str-2026-turno-breezeway-properly)).

## 3. KPIs
- **Occupancy** = booked nights ÷ available nights × 100 ([AirDNA](https://www.airdna.co/blog/short-term-rental-metrics)). AirDNA excludes host-blocked nights, while Key Data counts all nights ([Booked News](https://bookednews.com/data/short-term-rental-data-glossary/)).
- **ADR** = revenue ÷ booked nights. AirDNA includes the cleaning fee and excludes Airbnb service fees.
- **RevPAR** = occupancy × ADR = revenue ÷ available nights. Example: ADR $150 × 90% = $135 beats $200 × 60% = $120.
- **Lead time** = days from booking to check-in, averaged. **ALOS** = guest nights ÷ check-ins (Key Data).
- **Response rate** = new inquiries answered within 24 h over 30 days (Airbnb). Response time = median first reply (derived).
- **Review score**: Airbnb Superhost needs ≥4.8, counted once both reviews are in or after 14 days. Booking.com weights recent reviews since Jan 2025, keeps them 36 months, and invites reviews ~48 h after checkout ([Proptonomy](https://proptonomy.ai/research/guest-communication-2026)).
- **Cancellation rate** = cancelled ÷ reservations (Key Data). Superhost needs <1% host cancellations.
- **Direct share** = direct bookings (or revenue) ÷ total (derived).
- Ops extras: NOI = revenue − operating expenses, and turnover time (AirDNA).
- Definitions vary by vendor, so pin yours in-system.

## 4. Recommended message timeline
- **T0 booking confirmation (≤5 min)**: thanks, dates, what happens next, guidebook/FAQ link. Ask ETA/transport, guest count, **breakfast/dietary preferences** and occasion. Offer early check-in or a transfer, which converts best at this point. Last-minute bookings get the message immediately.
- **T−7 to −3 d (inference, no benchmark)**: nudge unanswered questions, house rules, balance/M-Pesa link and ID details (TRA register).
- **T−2 to −1 d (24–48 h)**: directions, landmarks, entrance/parking photos, check-in time, Wi-Fi, contact and quiet hours. Airbnb guests expect it ~48 h out ([Tellus](https://www.tellusapp.com/resources/passive-income/short-term-rentals/guest-communication-templates), [Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/resources/hosting-homes/a/using-quick-replies-to-save-time-73)).
- **Check-in day (morning)**: time-bound access code plus a keypad photo, and confirm ETA. Late arrivals (after 10 PM) get extra detail earlier ([Tellus](https://resources.tellusapp.com/passive-income/short-term-rentals/guest-communication-templates)).
- **+2–3 h after check-in**: "all good?"
- **Mid-stay (stays ≥4 nights, day 2–3)**: light check-in, tips and a mid-stay clean offer. Don't over-message.
- **Evening before checkout**: time, steps (keys, trash), a late-checkout offer if no same-day arrival, and breakfast final.
- **Checkout +1–2 h**: thanks, a specific mention, and a review request (Airbnb's window is 14 days).
- **+7–30 d (inference)**: rebooking offer, only to direct/non-Airbnb-sourced guests.

## Top 20 features a small-host system must have (ranked)
1. **Per-unit knowledge base feeding every channel** (prices, suites, breakfast, check-in, rules). 87% of pre-arrival questions are static, and 15.2% go unanswered when info is missing.
2. **Live availability and price quote by date/unit** across SMS/USSD/voice/web. This is the stated core challenge.
3. **Master calendar with conflict checks, and iCal import/export showing last-sync.** OTAs poll every 2–3 h.
4. **Triggered message timeline with placeholders** and last-minute handling.
5. **Pre-arrival capture of ETA, guests and breakfast/dietary preferences**, surfaced to the host and kitchen.
6. **Unified inbox plus FAQ auto-reply with a confidence threshold and escalation.** Facts are auto-answered, permissions go to the host, and problems stay human.
7. **Time-bound access codes** (igloohome/TTLock/Nuki, or a manual code bank) with hour-aligned validity.
8. **M-Pesa STK push plus card (3-D Secure), receipts, and deposit/balance tracking.**
9. **Turnover tasks on checkout with checklist and photo proof.** Cleaning is hosts' top time sink.
10. **Rule-based pricing** (base/min/max, season, weekend, last-minute, gap-night, length-of-stay, min-stay).
11. **Calendar-aware upsells** (early/late, breakfast, transfer, mid-stay clean), best offered at confirmation.
12. **Guest register, ID capture, TRA monthly-return export and ODPC consent/retention.**
13. **Multilingual templates/auto-translate** (English/Swahili first).
14. **Digital guidebook (QR in unit).**
15. **Review-request automation and score tracking** (Superhost ≥4.8).
16. **KPI dashboard** (occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, lead time, ALOS, response, cancellations, direct share).
17. **Direct-booking page and repeat-guest CRM** (Airbnb-safe).
18. **Maintenance log and work orders from guest messages.**
19. **Damage/incident evidence log and screening flags** (noise sensors optional).
20. **Statements with commission/levy estimates** (Airbnb 15.5%, Vrbo 12%, tourism levy 2%).
