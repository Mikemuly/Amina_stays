# Kenya / Mombasa context brief for Amina Stays (as of 25 Nov 2026)

⚠ marks anything uncertain, secondary-source only, or conflicting. Kenya Law blocks scrapers, so statutes are cited via TRA, Tourism Fund, KRA, ODPC or law-firm summaries.

**Bottom line**
- A multi-apartment host is a Class A tourism enterprise. That means a TRA licence per address, a 2% Tourism Levy, monthly TRA data returns, a guest register, ODPC registration and KRA tax.
- The licence number or QR code must now appear on every online listing. Licences expire 31 Dec 2026, so renewal is due now.
- Mombasa Airbnb guests are about 94% international (Airbtics), so you need cards, M-Pesa and multilingual messaging.
- Hackathon: Fri 27 Nov 2026, 08:00–17:30, Westerwelle Startup Haus, Mwembe Tayari, Mombasa, using Africa's Talking APIs (https://www.startupjobs.ke/events/4503-innovate-mombasa-hospitality-and-tourism-hackathon).

## 1. Regulation & compliance

### TRA / Tourism Act 2011
- **Class A scope.** Service apartments and flats, villas, holiday and beach cottages, homestays and guest houses need a TRA licence. Documents: registration certificate or CR12/ID, KRA PIN, manager CV, work permits for foreign directors (https://tra.go.ke/licensing-2/tra-licensing-class-a/).
- **Current rulebook** is the Tourism (Tourism Enterprises) Regulations 2025, LN 200, gazetted 16 Dec 2025 (I read the TRA-hosted PDF directly, https://tra.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/L.-N.-200-TOURISM-ENTERPRISES.pdf):
  - Licence is mandatory (r.15, r.24).
  - Counties may not issue a business permit without a TRA licence (r.15(3)).
  - Licence expires 31 Dec (r.19(5)).
  - It must be displayed on premises, and the serial number or QR must show on your own website, digital marketing and booking platforms (r.19(6)–(7)).
  - One licence per physical address (r.19(9)).
  - Late renewal costs 10% of the fee per month (r.20).
  - Unlicensed enterprises "shall not be listed on any digital marketing or booking platform" (r.24(3)).
  - Guest and staff records must be kept at least 5 years (r.26).
  - A monthly return of bed occupancy, visitors by country of origin and revenue is due (r.27).
  - Membership of a recognised tourism association is required "where applicable" (r.16–17).
- **Fees** (First Schedule, plus a one-off KES 1,000 application): service apartments 1 unit 12,000/yr, 2 units 20,000, 3–5 units 29,000, 6–10 units 38,000, 11–15 units 47,000, 16–20 units 56,000, 21+ units 65,000. Villas and beach cottages 37,000, holiday cottages 30,000, homestays 1,500–4,500 (same PDF).
- **Penalty baseline:** Tourism Act s.112 is up to KES 100,000 and/or 18 months (https://www.tourismfund.go.ke/offences-penalties/).
- ⚠ **Possible replacement regulations.** One 2026 outlet says "Tourism (Tourism Enterprises) Regulations, 2026" replaced the December rules, with fines up to KES 1m (https://streamlinefeed.co.ke/news/running-an-airbnb-in-kenya-licences-levies-taxes-bylaws).
  - Kenya Law lists LN 127 of 2026, with the date field showing "10 July 2025" (https://kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2026/127/eng@2025-07-10). I could not open it.
  - The same outlet says renewal must be filed two months before expiry (s.98(8)). I did not verify that.
  - Confirm both with TRA.
- **Verification:** guests can check licences at https://verify.tra.go.ke/ (TRA: 0111161200, info@tra.go.ke).
- **Enforcement is thin:** Airbtics found none of 3 Kenyan markets had more than 50% of listings showing licence data (https://airbtics.com/short-term-rental-market-report-2025-kenya).

### Tourism Levy (formerly "catering levy")
- It is 2% of gross receipts. The Tourism Fund succeeded the old Catering Levy Trustees. Returns and payment are due by the 10th of the following month via eLevy (eCitizen/M-Pesa), and records are kept 5 years (https://www.tourismfund.go.ke/faq/, https://venura.org/ke/catering-levy).
- It applies to Class A enterprises charging at least KES 250 per person (https://www.tourismfund.go.ke/registration/). "Serviced Apartments" are listed on the levy page (https://www.tourismfund.go.ke/levy/).
- **Formula:** levy = gross accommodation charge × 2/118 if VAT-registered with no service charge, × 2/102 if not VAT-registered, × 2/123 with a 5% service charge (https://www.tourismfund.go.ke/calculate-2-levy/).
- **Penalties:** failure to remit is up to KES 100,000 and/or 12 months (offences page above). ⚠ Late payment of KES 5,000 plus 3% a month appears in only one secondary source (venura link above).
- **Platform extension:** in Jan 2026 the government said the levy would extend to short-term-rental platforms, deducted at source, by end-June 2026 (https://techweez.com/2026/01/27/airbnb-short-term-rentals-tourism-levy/).
  - ⚠ By Sept 2026 it had not started because the enabling regulations were not gazetted (Business Daily, via https://www.airflowhq.com/blog/kenya-tourism-regulatory-authority-airbnb/).
  - If you are already TRA-licensed you owe it directly.

### County rules
- **Mombasa:** the Single Business Permit is applied for online via County e-services. A provisional permit is issued instantly and a final one follows inspection, valid for a calendar year (https://eprocedures.investkenya.go.ke/procedure/140/step/801?l=en). ⚠ I found no Mombasa-specific short-stay by-law.
- **Nairobi:** the 2024 County Assembly motion to regulate Airbnb had not become law by mid-2026 (https://manwaadvocates.com/operating-an-airbnb-in-kenya-legal-requirements-taxes-and-compliance-guide/). ⚠ The Streamline article above says Nairobi's Finance Act 2023 sets ABNB licence fees of KES 35,000–120,000. I could not verify that.
- **Kisumu** directed Airbnb registration in Jan 2024 (https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/data-hub/kenya-s-short-term-rental-sector-stands-at-crossroads--4501068).

### KRA tax
- **Airbnb withholding:** Airbnb withholds on gross host earnings at 5% if you give a valid KRA PIN and 20% if not. This applies to residents and non-residents, and the PIN must match your name (https://www.airbnb.co.uk/help/article/4076). ⚠ The withheld tax is probably creditable on your annual return (https://pennycompass.com/ke/digital-marketplace-tax-calculator).
- **Rental income tax (MRI):** 7.5% final tax on gross residential rent of roughly KES 288k–15m a year for resident landlords, in force since 1 Jan 2024 (https://www.kra.go.ke/individual/filing-paying/types-of-taxes/residential-rental-income).
  - ⚠ A serviced short-stay is often treated as business income taxed on profit, and KRA guidance does not address nightly lets (https://maj.co.ke/airbnb-tax-in-kenya.html). Ask your accountant.
  - ⚠ Finance Act 2026 reportedly left MRI unchanged (https://probityfinance.co.ke/insights/rental-income-tax-landlords-airbnb-kenya-2026.html).
- **VAT:** 16% on accommodation once taxable supplies reach KES 5m in 12 months. The VAT Act "hotel" definition covers service apartments, cottages and villas, and stays of a month or more fall outside it (Streamline link above, ⚠ single source).
- **Finance Act 2025:** eTIMS invoicing became mandatory (https://adamjeeauditors.com/kenyan-finance-act-2025-2026/, ⚠ summary).
- **Platform tax (not the host's):** the Significant Economic Presence (SEP) tax replaced the 1.5% DST in Dec 2024. Finance Act 2025 widened it and dropped the KES 5m threshold (https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2026/10/kenya-final-regulations-significant-economic-presence-tax.html). Final regs are LN 188 of 2026, with an effective rate of about 3% of Kenyan gross turnover (⚠ per CDH: https://www.cliffedekkerhofmeyr.com/news/publications/2026/Kenya/Tax-Exchange-Control/tax-and-exchange-control-alert-22-october-Significant-economic-presence-tax-New-regulations-for-foreign-digital-businesses).
- **Enforcement:** KRA's eRITS (April 2025) cross-matches property and tenant data. On 28 Oct 2026 KRA told MPs it plans to use M-Pesa till and Pochi la Biashara data to find undeclared income (https://nairobibusinessmonthly.com/kra-flags-over-one-million-landlords-over-tax-evasion/).

### Guest register / ID / police
- **PSRA directive, 15 Jan 2024** (s.48 Private Security Regulation Act), after two Nairobi murders (https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/world/2024-01-15-state-issues-new-rules-to-lodging-facilities-after-two-deaths):
  - Guards must log ID details and entry/exit times for every visitor.
  - ID is held temporarily, returned at exit, and used for nothing else.
  - A vehicle log is required.
  - CCTV must be working and recording.
  - An access-control policy must be displayed, and an occurrence book kept.
- The Interior Ministry added that Airbnbs must register with TRA and install CCTV (https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2024-01-31-its-now-mandatory-for-guards-to-take-airbnb-client-details). The 5-year guest record is TRA r.26.
- ⚠ I found no separate immigration or police guest-form rule.

### Data Protection Act 2019 / ODPC
- **Registration:** "hospitality industry firms" (excluding tour guides) must register as data controllers regardless of turnover (https://www.cliffedekkerhofmeyr.com/news/publications/2022/Practice/TMT/tmt-alert-14-february-Part-3-Unpacking-the-Data-Protection-Registration-of-Data-Controllers-and-Data-Processors-Regulations-of-2021.html).
  - Fee is KES 4,000 for micro/small, valid 24 months, KES 2,000 to renew. A property manager acting for owners is a processor (https://dataprivacyadvocates.co.ke/resources/registration-guides/hospitality/).
- **Rights:** access within 7 days and erasure within 14 days (General Regs 2021, https://kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2021/263/eng@2022-01-14/source.pdf).
- **Other duties:** breach notice to ODPC within 72 hours (s.43), parental consent for children's data (s.33), consent for commercial use (s.37), and fines up to KES 5m or 1% of turnover (https://www.priviq.com/global-privacy-law-guide/kenya-dpa-2019/).
- **Foreign cloud hosting** is a cross-border transfer needing safeguards and a data processing agreement (https://difaconsultancy.com/resources/guidance-notes-for-cross-border-data-transfers/).
- **ID capture:** the PSRA "temporary withholding" is purpose-limited and does not override data minimisation (https://dataprivacyadvocates.co.ke/resources/registration-guides/hospitality/).
- **Enforcement is real:** The Legend Hotel was ordered to pay KES 250,000 in 2025 (https://dataprotection.co.ke/determinations/odpc-comp-466-2025). A pizza chain paid KES 250,000 for promo SMS without notice (https://research.lawlab.africa/tracker/kenya/odpc/2025/pepino-pizza/). A lender paid KES 400,000 (https://research.lawlab.africa/tracker/kenya/odpc/2025/platinum-credit-limited/).

### SMS marketing
- Reg 17 of the CA Consumer Protection Regs 2010: automated direct marketing without prior consent is an offence. Schemes must be opt-in, with a free and simple opt-out (https://kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2010/54).
- **Sender IDs** are registered per network, and Safaricom splits promotional from transactional IDs. One ID cannot carry both. Promo IDs are limited to 08:00–18:00 and need opt-out text. A one-way sender ID cannot receive "STOP", so use a link or a two-way keyword (https://esmsafrica.io/blog/sms-marketing-law-kenya-odpc, https://esmsafrica.io/blog/sender-id-registration-compared-africa).
- ⚠ Promo windows are reported differently as 07:00–19:00 (https://www.broadnet.me/blog/kenya-bulk-sms-sender-id-regulations-2026) and 08:00–20:00 (https://kenyasms.com/blog/cak-sms-regulations-kenya-compliance). Use the strictest.
- ⚠ Approval time is reported as 5–7 business days up to about 4 weeks, so you cannot register an ID before the hackathon.
- ⚠ I found no primary CA enforcement notice on bulk SMS for 2024–25. Context: Airtel flagged 68m suspicious messages in Kenya (https://thesharpdaily.com/sms-spam-surge-kenya-data-misuse-telcos/), and CA fraud and unsolicited-message complaints are rising (https://connectmedia.co.ke/reports/sms-fraud-kenya-2026/).

- **Mombasa, 19 Jan 2025:** a gang posing as hosts lured two Russians to a Kisauni Airbnb. They forced a RUB 1.99m transfer plus cash, and three suspects were arrested (https://www.newsmoto.co.ke/robbery-with-violence-suspects-arrested-in-nyali-mombasa/).

- **Child protection:** the US 2025 TIP report says traffickers increasingly use private villas and vacation homes, including in Mombasa. Child sex tourism carries at least 10 years and a KES 2m fine (https://www.ecoi.net/en/document/2130642.html).
- ⚠ I could not verify any named Kenyan Airbnb or short-stay hosts association. The Coast hoteliers' body is KAHC Coast (https://businessradar.co.ke/kenya-tourist-arrivals-2-8-million-hotels-full/). A pan-African network exists at https://africanhostsnetwork.com/.

## 3. Payments
- **M-Pesa FY26:** 40.99m one-month active customers. Revenue was KES 182.7bn, transaction value KES 41.68tn and volume 46.41bn (https://docs.publicnow.com/viewDoc?filename=33864%5CEXT%5CCDDF378667EF11893411A67A9F47CA2FCFF1003C_79AF7073926B9C9E8050E6E683A088F8E40B2986.PDF).
- ⚠ CBK reports 473,536 active agents (+24%) but monthly transaction counts down 30% (https://www.ecofinagency.com/news-digital/2409-59189-kenya-saw-30-drop-in-mobile-money-transactions-in-2025-despite-more-users). That conflicts with Safaricom's volume growth, probably a definitional difference.
- **Daraja 3.0** launched 25 Nov 2025: cloud-native, security APIs and mini-apps in the M-PESA app (https://www.safaricom.co.ke/media-center-landing/press-releases/safaricom-launches-daraja-3-0-to-enhance-security-speed-and-developer-innovation). ⚠ Secondary sources say the STK Push endpoint and callbacks are unchanged (https://dev.to/ronnyabuto/what-daraja-30-actually-changed-for-developers-and-what-it-did-not-3ek4). Result codes: 1032 cancelled, 1037 timeout (https://www.devaltech.co.ke/insights/daraja-3-what-changed-for-your-mpesa-payments).
- **Go-live** needs your own Paybill or Till, roughly 2–3 weeks per one vendor (https://www.shortstayskenya.com/mpesa-payments-for-airbnb).
- **Aggregators (⚠ indicative fees):** Flutterwave about 1.4% local and 3.8% international, Pesapal 1.5–3.5%, Paystack about 1.95% and 3.9%. DPO is strongest for travel and multi-currency cards, and some gateways hold 5–10% reserves on travel merchants (https://paymentproviders.io/blog/best-payment-gateways-kenya, https://www.domaintechhubs.com/insights/pesapal-vs-flutterwave-vs-dpo-vs-paystack-kenya).
- **Africa's Talking Payments** has mobile checkout (STK), B2C and B2B (https://apis.apievangelist.com/store/africastalking-payments-api/).
- **Foreigners:** M-Pesa needs a Safaricom SIM registered in person with a passport (⚠ guides only, https://mobilemoney.co.ke/guides/how-to-use-m-pesa-as-a-tourist-or-foreigner-in-kenya-complete-setup-guide/). The M-PESA GlobalPay Visa virtual card is for Kenyans paying abroad, not for tourists paying you (https://www.safaricom.co.ke/media-center-landing/press-releases/safaricom-and-visa-launch-m-pesa-globalpay-visa-virtual-card). Use a card gateway for tourists.
- **Airbnb payouts:** bank account (3–5 business days), Fast Pay (≤30 min), international wire, Payoneer, PayPal or Western Union. Availability varies by country (https://www.airbnb.com/resources/hosting-homes/a/how-youll-get-paid-for-hosting-20). ⚠ I found no evidence of a native M-Pesa payout.
- **Fraud:** fake M-Pesa SMS with a "*LOCKED*" balance and fake-reversal scams are common (https://mobilemoney.co.ke/guides/mpesa-scams/). Confirm payments via callback only.

## 4. Market data
- **Arrivals 2025:** 2,550,641 excluding Kenyans (+6.2%) and 2.65m including diaspora. Earnings were KES 501.3bn. Mombasa airport handled 235,184 arrivals (9.2%). Beach-hotel bed-nights were 4.94m, up from 4.14m. National hotel occupancy was 29.3% (https://www.globaltourismstatistics.com/kenya).
  - The Ministry reports 2.7m international arrivals and about KES 0.5tn (https://magicalkenya.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/KENYAS-TOURISM-SECTOR-ACHIEVES-STRONG-GROWTH-IN-2025-RECORDING-Ksh.-0.5-TRILLION-IN-EARNINGS_final-1.pdf).
  - Jan–Jun 2026 arrivals were 1.23m, up 7.6%.
- **Top markets 2025:** US 304k, Uganda 235k, Tanzania 210k, UK 171k, India 113k, Italy 107k (+19.9%), China 95k, Germany 86k. Most Italians (77k) arrive via Mombasa (globaltourismstatistics link above).
- **Coast capacity and bookings:** bed capacity rose from about 30k to 50k with apartments, villas and short-stays. Coast hotels report being full through December 2026. Hoteliers worry about the 2027 election and want more direct flights (https://businessradar.co.ke/kenya-tourist-arrivals-2-8-million-hotels-full/).
- **Seasonality:**
  - Coast: high Dec–Mar (calm, dry), cheapest Apr–Jun (long rains), short rains in Nov, Christmas about 2× price (https://dianikenya.org/best-time-to-visit-diani-beach/, https://authenticadventures.co.ke/guides/best-time-to-visit-mombasa/).
  - National: Jul–Aug peak, Mar–Apr quietest (globaltourismstatistics link above).
  - Ramadan 2027 is expected to start ~8 Feb (moon-sighting dependent), with Eid ~9–10 March (⚠ https://ramadancountdown2027.com/kenya/).
- **SGR** carried 2.73m passengers in 2025 (https://french.news.cn/20260211/20bf907e095c4ff69e60d6084301f4e2/c.html). Nairobi–Mombasa fares are KES 1,500 economy, 4,500 first and 12,000 premium. ⚠ Older sources say 1,000/3,000. The terminus is at Miritini, about 12 km from the centre (https://travelwithmkay.co.ke/sgr-nairobi-to-mombasa/).
- **Moi International Airport:** 1,718,674 passengers in 2025, against a 2.5m capacity (https://travelnews.africa/news-single.html?id=18496). Carriers include Condor, Discover, Neos, TUI, Enter Air, flydubai and Ethiopian. Turkish is capped at 3 flights a week (https://www.travelandtourworld.com/news/article/shatyj8yus9f/).
- **Ferry and south coast:** the Dongo Kundu bypass gives airport to Diani in about 45–60 minutes, versus Likoni ferry queues of 30 minutes to hours (https://dianikenya.org/getting-here/likoni-ferry/). Locals still use the ferry because of fuel cost (https://mjengohub.co.ke/articles/infrastructure/mombasa-motorists-stick-to-likoni-ferry-as-fuel-costs-hit-dongo-kundu-bypass-usage).
- **eTA:** costs USD 30 (https://entrycardguide.com/kenya/). Since July 2025 all African nationals except Somalia and Libya are exempt, and EAC citizens get up to 180 days (https://www.cliffedekkerhofmeyr.com/news/publications/2025/Sectors/Immigration-Law/immigration-law-alert-24-july-kenya-Kenya-amends-immigration-rules-broadens-eTA-exemptions-for-African-and-other-nationals?geo=KE).
- **Languages (my inference ⚠):** English and Kiswahili first. Then Italian, German, Polish, Dutch, Arabic, Hindi/Gujarati and Chinese, based on markets and charter carriers.
- **Short-stay market, Mombasa:**
  - AirDNA (Oct 2026): 1,873 active listings, 41% occupancy, ADR $58, about $8K/yr. ⚠ Listings are down 47% year on year (https://www.airdna.co/vacation-rental-data/app/ke/default/mombasa/overview).
  - Airbtics 2025: 38% occupancy, ADR $47, $6,629 revenue, supply +53.5%, 94% international guests (Airbtics link above).
  - ⚠ Another source says about 3,700 listings (https://realtors.co.ke/is-airbnb-profitable-in-kenya-a-2025-2026-data-driven-analysis-for-property-investors/).

## 5. Coastal operations
- **Power:** near-nationwide blackout on 9 Dec 2025 (Kenya–Uganda interconnector). On 5 Nov 2025 President Ruto acknowledged 5–10 pm rationing in some regions (https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/118905-kenya-power-issues-alert-after-widespread-blackout-hits-nairobi-western-rift-valley). Planned Coast outages are listed at https://kplc.project.co.ke/power-interruptions/region/coast-region (unofficial aggregator).
- **Water:** Coast demand is 512,000 m³/day against 185,000 supply (42%). Mwache Dam was 86% complete, with impounding from October 2026 and +186,000 m³/day later (https://eastleighvoice.co.ke/news/369889/coast-counties-grappling-with-327000-cubic-metres-daily-water-deficit-cs-mugaa). The treatment plant is not built yet, so relief is not immediate (https://www.kenyatribune.com/government-expedites-mwache-dam-construction-to-counter-water-shortages/).
- **Security:**
  - FCDO flags heightened terror and kidnap threat including coastal counties, plus violent crime (https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/kenya/safety-and-security).
  - KWS reinforced Tourism Police on beaches this festive season (https://www.kbc.co.ke/tight-security-in-mombasa-assured-as-festive-season-kicks-off/).
- **Beach boys and scams:** touts offer tours and "friendship", then pressure for money. Book excursions through your host or hotel (⚠ guide sources, https://arrivalbrief.com/cities/mombasa/, https://tabiji.ai/scams/mombasa/).
- **Transport:** Uber, Bolt and Little operate. A 5 km ride costs about KES 300–600, and tuk-tuk fares should be agreed first (⚠ https://solosquare.app/getting-around/mombasa). Airport taxis are about KES 1,500 to town (https://www.gate2.city/airports/mba).
- **Culture:** cover shoulders and knees in Old Town. Non-Muslims should not enter mosques without invitation. Avoid eating or drinking in public during Ramadan (https://www.thekenyaguide.com/practical/kenya-etiquette-and-customs/, https://www.kenia-reisen.com/reisefuehrer/sehenswuerdigkeit/altstadt-von-mombasa).
- **Breakfast:** mahamri with mbaazi za nazi (pigeon peas in coconut), spiced ginger/cardamom chai, vitumbua, kaimati, chapati and mandazi (https://www.kenyaexperience.org/food/kenyan-breakfast). Viazi karai is a well-known coastal snack, uncited.
- **Emergency:**
  - 999 or 112 (112 works without airtime). Kenya Red Cross 1199. E-Plus ambulance 1514 or 0700 395 395. St John Ambulance 0721 225 285 (Mombasa 0706 777 077). AMREF Flying Doctors +254 20 699 2299 (https://businessradar.co.ke/emergency-contacts-for-ambulance-services-in-kenya-every-number-you-need-to-have/, https://infohub.co.ke/kenya-emergency-contacts/).
  - ⚠ No verified Tourism Police hotline. Directory numbers are unverified: 041-2222622 (https://www.cybo.com/KE-biz/the-kenya-police-tourist-unit_1g) and 041-2222121 (https://hersafevoyage.com/destinations/kenya/mombasa).

## Top 15 Kenya-specific requirements/features the platform should include
1. **TRA licence vault:** per-address licence number and QR, expiry alerts (31 Dec), 10%-a-month late-fee warning, and auto-insertion into listings and booking pages (r.15, 19, 20, 24).
2. **Digital guest register:** all occupants, including minors, with ID/passport number, nationality, phone, vehicle plate and in/out times. Retain 5 years (r.26) and export it for police or TRA.
3. **Monthly TRA return generator:** bed occupancy, visitors by country, revenue (r.27).
4. **Tourism Levy engine:** 2/118, 2/102 or 2/123 formulas, the 10th-of-month reminder and an eLevy-ready export.
5. **Tax pack:** KRA PIN capture (Airbnb 5% versus 20% withholding), channel versus direct-M-Pesa split, eTIMS invoices, a VAT KES 5m threshold tracker, and MRI-versus-business-income notes.
6. **M-Pesa STK Push** (Daraja 3.0 or Africa's Talking) for deposits and balances, using callbacks as the only proof of payment. Add reconciliation and B2C refunds, and warn guests about fake SMS.
7. **Foreign-tourist card path** (DPO, Pesapal, Flutterwave or Paystack) in USD/EUR/GBP, with payment links sent by SMS, WhatsApp or email.
8. **Safety-by-design check-in:** pre-arrival ID upload, a guard-facing visitor log (PSRA s.48), CCTV and access-control checklist, an SOS keyword by SMS/USSD, and prominent 999/112/1199/1514.
